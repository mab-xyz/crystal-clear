This folder contains analyses, scripts, and visualizations used during research of [On-Chain Analysis of Smart Contract
Dependency Risks on Ethereum](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.19548).

This paper mainly answer four questions:
1. What is the scale of smart contract dependencies in the field?
2. Which are the most prolific creators of smart contracts?
3. Which smart contracts are most frequently called by other contracts?
4. How transparent are smart contract interactions in major DeFi protocols?
